Ghana Assistant: landmark-based directions for Accra and Kumasi, and short answers about Ghana grounded in news and research.
One question, one answer (no chat). The model runs on Modal; set the `ASSISTANT_URL` secret to its `/ask` endpoint.
Code: https://github.com/GhanaNLP/ghana-assistant
